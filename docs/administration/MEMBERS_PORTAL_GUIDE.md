# Members Portal Guide

## Entry

Open `/members/login/` and sign in with the account invited by the club. Public signup is disabled. Password recovery is available from the login screen.

## Dashboard

After successful sign-in, open `/members/dashboard/`. Active invited members, committee members, ordinary BOD members and office bearers use the same member-level portal.

The dashboard retains profile management, attendance, events and registrations, assigned tasks, contributions, announcements, documents, achievements, leaderboard, member directory and settings.

## Permissions

Members may view and update permitted profile fields, review their own attendance, register for events, view assigned tasks, submit contributions, read authorized club announcements/documents and view achievements.

Members cannot invite users, assign roles, change permissions, approve contributions, manage other members' attendance or records, access admin analytics or audit logs, or perform administrative RPC operations.

## Access problems

Accounts must be invited and have an active membership profile. Suspended or inactive members are blocked. Contact a club office bearer if the invitation, membership status or password needs attention.
