# Members Portal — Member Guide

## Sign in

Use the invitation sent to your verified email. Public registration is disabled. Sign in at `/members/login/`. Existing Auth accounts must use the email address matching the pending club invitation and accept that invitation from their dashboard.

## Your dashboard

Open `/members/dashboard/` to access your profile, attendance, events and registrations, tasks, contributions, announcements, documents, achievements, leaderboard, member directory and settings. General members, committee members, ordinary BOD members and office bearers use the same member-level portal.

## Permissions

You can update permitted profile fields, view your attendance, register for events, view assigned tasks, submit contributions, read authorized announcements/documents and view achievements. You cannot invite members, assign roles, approve contributions, manage other members' attendance or records, or access administrative analytics and audit logs.

## Account help

Use Forgot password from the login screen. Suspended/inactive membership and invitation issues must be referred to a club office bearer through the club's official contact channel.
